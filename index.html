Who we are

A community dedicated to empowering future leaders.

AGSM Women in Business (WIB) brings together aspiring leaders at UC Riverside for networking, workshops, and events that build real connections. From mentorship meetups to career-building sessions, case practice, and community events, WIB is where members grow their skills and their circle — together.

Fall Kickoff

Meeting members where they were.

We kicked off the year tabling across campus — pins, props, and #girlboss photo-booth signs included — introducing WIB to students discovering the club for the first time.

Halloween Movie Night

Monster House, and a lot of costumes.

WIB teamed up with SAB for a Halloween Movie Night at the Hillside Garden Terrace — costumes, snacks, a bake sale, and a screening of Monster House to scare away those midterm worries.

Info Session

Kicking off 2025–26.

We opened the year with our Info Session, introducing new and returning members to WIB's 2025–26 leadership team and everything the club had planned for the year ahead.

She Leads

A panel on leading with purpose.

WIB hosted a "She Leads" panel discussion featuring AGSM's Lori Barogian (Director, Graduate Student Success) and Erika Santoyo (Graduate Programs Enrollment Manager), sharing career advice and lessons in leadership with our members.

Secret Santa

Holiday cheer, one gift at a time.

Our winter Secret Santa brought the holiday spirit to WIB — thoughtful gifts, good company, and a table full of surprises to close out the quarter.
